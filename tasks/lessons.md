# Lessons

- Preserve platform neutrality in product language: deployment, integrations, identity, repositories, and AI models remain customer-selected.
- Keep fictional transaction content visibly labeled and avoid realistic financial, personal, legal, donor, or customer information.
- Describe safeguards as configurable controls, not guarantees or claims of compliance.
- Treat AI outputs as permission-bound drafts that require human review before disclosure, publication, or redaction.
- Avoid inflated language in RFI, RFP, proposal, and product copy, including “comprehensive,” “seamless,” “guaranteed,” and similar superlatives.
